About Me

I've been playing games since I was five, from Minecraft and Growtopia to Roblox and Valorant, and that's what pushed me to start making them. I began on Roblox in 2020, designing clothing and modelling UGC in Blender, and have since moved into game systems, logic and sound design.

Rhythm games like osu! and Project Sekai showed me how closely sound and gameplay work together. As a DJ and dancer, I use rhythm, energy and atmosphere to shape how a game feels.

Outside of games, I volunteer with youth and senior initiatives and marched in NDP 2024 with Goodhood.sg.

AI-Assisted

Bassline Break

Web (HTML5) · Web Audio · itch.io (2026)
Solo · Built with Claude Code & Gemini

A 4-key rhythm game inspired by Project SEKAI and RoBeats. Every note you hit plays part of the melody, so you perform the song as you play it.

  • How I used AI: directed Claude Code to build the game, and used Gemini to write and refine my prompts
  • Design: 3 tracks (128–136 BPM), 3 difficulties (Chill, Groove, Club), tap and hold notes, S-rank scoring
  • Audio: all sound is generated live with Web Audio; hits play synth notes locked to the song's key
  • Plays anywhere: desktop (D F J K) and mobile touch, with audio latency calibration

AI-assisted code, graphics and text, as disclosed on itch.io.
