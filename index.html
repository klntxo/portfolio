About Me

My journey into game creation started in 2020 through Roblox, where I taught myself 3D modeling in Blender to design UGC concepts and digital fashion. Since then, my passion has expanded into interactive systems, game logic, and sound design.

Music is deeply tied to how I craft player experiences. As a DJ and dancer, I leverage rhythm, energy flow, and atmospheric audio to guide player emotions and shape spatial "vibes" within games.

Beyond technical and creative work, I value community involvement—from marching in NDP 2024 with Goodhood.sg to youth and senior volunteer initiatives. I strive to bring energy, empathy, and attention to detail into everything I build.
